# v0.4.0 Release Prep And Forward Roadmap

## Summary

- The eight planned `0.4.0` credential and danmaku PRs have landed on `master`.
- `v0.4.0` moved through the protected release candidate and promotion workflow.
- Follow-up product work is split into later release lines so the `0.4.0` release remains focused
  on credential profiles, access-key acquisition, login QR output, credential health checks, and
  append-only danmaku updates.

## Current State

- `Cargo.toml` and package metadata shipped on the `0.4.0` line.
- GitHub Releases and crates.io now show shipped versions through `v0.4.0`.
- `docs/PROJECT_STATE.md` now points to this release-prep note as a completed recovery entry and to
  the `v0.5.0` roadmap as the active follow-up.
- `docs/PROJECT_TODO.md` records the protected `v0.4.0` release as completed and captures the
  follow-up release boundaries.

## Planned Follow-Up Lines

- `v0.5.0`: downloader and embedding polish, including embedder progress callbacks,
  cancellation-aware download execution, chapter metadata muxing, audio language selection, and AI
  subtitle filtering.
- `v0.6.0`: credential lifecycle, including automatic credential refresh, health policy/reporting,
  profile-level status, and multi-account lifecycle UX.
- `v0.7.0` or later feed/page work: per-video related recommendations and additional Bilibili page
  families that do not fit the downloader or credential lines.
- Remaining BBDown parity backlog stays available for reprioritization: aria2 or multi-thread
  download integration, MP4Box muxing, and subtitle-to-SRT conversion.

## Next Steps

- Continue with the `v0.5.0` downloader and embedding polish roadmap.
